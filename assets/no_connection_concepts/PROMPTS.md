# Imagegen prompts

All ten images were created with the built-in imagegen tool. In every prompt,
Image 1 was `assets/p2000t_no_connection_sprite.png` and served only as the
P2000T subject reference.

## 01 — Isometric 8-bit grid

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: 640x480 embedded VGA no-connection screen concept
Primary request: Create a bold 8-bit isometric pixel-art tribute to the P2000T in Image 1, designed from the outset for an extremely small palette.
Input images: Image 1 is a subject reference only.
Scene/backdrop: black space with a sparse cyan perspective grid and a few chunky signal-break pixels.
Subject: simplified recognizable cream-and-black P2000T with yellow keyboard, shown isometrically as a single large central sprite.
Style/medium: authentic early 1980s 8-bit computer-game splash screen, large deliberate pixels, hard 1-bit edges, no antialiasing, flat fills, selective checkerboard dithering.
Composition/framing: 4:3 landscape; quiet dark band across the top 12 percent and bottom 18 percent for firmware text and status icon; subject centered in the middle.
Color palette: at most 12 visually distinct colors chosen from black, cream, gold, cyan, navy, gray and white.
Constraints: no readable text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no fine photographic texture, no gradients; every important feature must survive at 640x480 and 16-color RGB444 quantization.
Avoid: photorealism, soft shadows, smooth surfaces, tiny keyboard labels, dense noise.
```
